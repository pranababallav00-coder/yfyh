LUDO

2 to 4 players on one screen. Pass the device around the table.

Rules in short
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard.
  • Move clockwise around the board, then up your own coloured lane to the centre.
  • You need the exact number to reach the centre.
  • Land on another colour to send it back to its yard, unless it stands on a star or a coloured start square.
  • A 6, a capture, or a token reaching the centre gives another roll.
  • Three 6s in a row cancel your turn.
  • First to bring all 4 tokens home wins. The game goes on until everyone is ranked.
